The original GLSL was ported to HLSL (Shader Model 6.5) and modified for the
SeraphicRT Minecraft RTX sky:
  - step count: the 50-layer march is sampled with a configurable number of
    stratified layers (SERAPHIC_AURORA_STEPS) whose index, colour progression
    and accumulation weights are remapped to the 50-layer integral;
  - jitter: the per-pixel gl_FragCoord hash was replaced by interleaved-gradient
    noise on a direction-stable pixel grid with a golden-ratio frame offset,
    resolved by temporal anti-aliasing;
  - HDR scaling: the display-referred output is converted to scene-linear
    radiance calibrated to the game's night exposure and tone mapper, with a
    visibility control (SERAPHIC_AURORA_VISIBILITY);
  - sky integration: evaluated as night-sky emission in the physical sky
    (night, rain and horizon gates, atmospheric transmittance, veiling the
    stars, hidden by clouds); the original background, star field and water
    reflection code are not used.
